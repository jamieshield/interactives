(Approximate) HCT

This prototype is used to motivate students to move from RGB to HCT by helping them understand how a colour palette can be generated using HCT. The code is genAI.

For example, first, choose a seed hue. Your primary colours could be dark tone text contrasted on light tone background. Your secondary colours could use a nearby, analogous hue with subdued chroma. Again you will use a dark toned text contrasted on a light toned background.

  • Tone: use large adjustments of Tone for readability by generating a foreground text colour that contrasts with its background
  • Hue: small adjustments of the primary Hue provide harmonious, analogous, secondary colours
  • Chroma: reducing the chroma produces subdued secondary colours which allows the user's attention to be directed to the more important primary elements.
